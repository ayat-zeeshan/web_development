Early Life & Education

Joanne Rowling was born on July 31, 1965, near Bristol, England at Yate General Hospital. She grew up in Gloucestershire and Chepstow, Wales. Her parents were Peter Rowling (an aircraft engineer at Rolls Royce), and Anne, her mother (a science technician). As for siblings, there is her younger sister Di, born in 1967, and deeply loved by Joanne. Her full name is Dianne and the two sisters shared a close bond through their childhood.

J.K Rowling attended St. Micheals Primary School, located in Winterbourne, Gloucestershire. This was where she developed her huge interest in literacy (typically reading and writing). At the age of eleven, she began her secondary education at Wyeden School and College. She was further inspired by teachers who helped her improve precision in writing and enhance her level of imagination. There has also been reports of Rowling mentioning tht her secondary school has delivered inspiration for some characters in Harry Potter series, like Hermione Granger.

Her higher education took place at the University of Exeter, where she studied French and Classics. She spent a year in Paris due to her coursework, broadening her linguistic and cultural knowledge, and Classics included lot of Latin which later put influence into Harry Potter spells. She graduated in 1986 and gained knowledge on human rights and global problems in her time of working at Amnesty International in London. Then in 1997, she earned a BA in French.
